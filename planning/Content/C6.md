Advanced American Academy offers a plethora of programs, hosting age groups from toddlers to 12th grade. Starting with our Toddler-Kindergarten levels, we use a hands-on learning approach, creating a space safe for exploration and an open learning environment. We encourage our little learners to explore and interact with the environment around them, allowing them to learn and comfortably grow through every interaction. Our elementary program focuses on building strong academic foundations for our students, setting them up for future success. Our classes encourage students to deeply engage with state aligned curriculum, develop crucial problem-solving skills, and learn how to apply their knowledge in meaningful ways. In this group, we also teach our students how to deal and approach the world around them with empathy and understanding, through collaborative and meaningful projects. Our middle school program prepares students for advanced learning, emphasizes personal growth, social responsibility, and emotional intelligence. High expectations are set for our middle schoolers so that they are prepared to excel in their future high school courses. Our middle school program has an increased focus on discipline, critical thinking, analysis, and collaboration. Our high school program pushes our students to try their best, through challenging and rigorous coursework. We provide a multitude of High School courses, allowing students to explore different areas of interest. Our high school offers a blend of academic challenges as well as personal growth, allowing students to take on larger, more fulfilling projects.

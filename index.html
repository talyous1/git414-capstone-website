About Us

Advanced American Academy is one of Houston's largest accredited private schools, featuring a three-story, fully equipped, state-of-the art building, and multiple sports fields. We nurture academic excellence and character building, through combining rigorous education with values such as empathy and understanding. Guided by our mission to develop positive forces for change, we prepare students to lead with purpose and understanding. Advanced American Academy upholds national standards through its accreditation, and empower students to thrive across the United States and beyond.

School Offerings

Advanced American Academy offers a plethora of programs, hosting age groups from toddlers to 12th grade.

Toddler – Kindergarten

Starting with our Toddler-Kindergarten levels, we use a hands-on learning approach, creating a space safe for exploration and an open learning environment. We encourage our little learners to explore and interact with the environment around them, allowing them to learn and comfortably grow through every interaction.

Elementary School

Our elementary program focuses on building strong academic foundations for our students, setting them up for future success. Our classes encourage students to deeply engage with state aligned curriculum, develop crucial problem-solving skills, and learn how to apply their knowledge in meaningful ways. In this group, we also teach our students how to deal and approach the world around them with empathy and understanding, through collaborative and meaningful projects.

Middle School

Our middle school program prepares students for advanced learning, emphasizes personal growth, social responsibility, and emotional intelligence. High expectations are set for our middle schoolers so that they are prepared to excel in their future high school courses. Our middle school program has an increased focus on discipline, critical thinking, analysis, and collaboration.

High School

Our high school program pushes our students to try their best, through challenging and rigorous coursework. We provide a multitude of High School courses, allowing students to explore different areas of interest. Our high school offers a blend of academic challenges as well as personal growth, allowing students to take on larger, more fulfilling projects.

About Us

Advanced American Academy is one of Houston's largest accredited private schools, featuring a three-story, fully equipped, state-of-the art building, and multiple sports fields. We nurture academic excellence and character building, through combining rigorous education with values such as empathy and understanding. Guided by our mission to develop positive forces for change, we prepare students to lead with purpose and understanding. Advanced American Academy upholds national standards through its accreditation, and empower students to thrive across the United States and beyond.

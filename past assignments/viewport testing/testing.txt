I tested my website at 1280x855, 768x1024, and 375x667 through Chrome DevTools. In the 1280 viewport width, program cards display in three columns. The paragraphs split into three on the first row and hold the same height even with different paragraph lengths. The feature grid displays at two columns with the photo and table side-by-side. In the 768 viewport width, the cards reduced to  two columns, without having to use a media query! The subgrid still allowed the middle school and high school to align on one baseline. In the 375 viewport widths, everything combined into one single column, without any horizontal scroll issues. This was done through the min(18rem, 100%) guard. The fit-content and nowrap on the date column also worked as "August 10th, 2026" stayed on one line.

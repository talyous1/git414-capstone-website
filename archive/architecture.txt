I used seven layers to organize my CSS; reset, base, layout, components, utilities, states, and overrides. The layers are in order of least specific to most overriding. I separated the tokens into groups of color, type, spacing, width, shape, and focus. I set different spacing levels. I created --button-bg and --button-text, which both are located in .button instead of :root, as they only need to be defined within the button styling. I name utilities according to what they are for and components for what they are. Selectors are mostly single-class. As for browser checks, I do not have html yet.
